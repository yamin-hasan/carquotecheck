COMMON QUESTIONS

Arkansas car-buying FAQs

Plain-English context. Always confirm the final figures on your buyer's order.

What is the dealer doc fee limit in Arkansas?

The current reference record lists a confirmed statewide dealer doc-fee cap of $129 for Arkansas. Some caps depend on dealer type, filing method, or transaction conditions. Compare the contract line with the official rule linked in this guide before disputing a charge.

Does Arkansas give a tax credit for a trade-in?

A qualifying dealer trade-in generally reduces the taxable amount in Arkansas. Eligibility can depend on ownership, timing, titling, and how the sale is written. Enter your trade value and confirm that the dealer applied the credit on the purchase worksheet rather than relying only on a verbal estimate.

What vehicle sales tax rate should I enter for Arkansas?

This calculator begins with a 6.5% Arkansas state-level vehicle-tax rate. It may not include county, city, district, or vehicle-specific taxes. Change the rate to match the dealer's written worksheet, and compare the taxable base as well as the percentage so a trade-in or add-on is not counted twice.

Is a dealer doc fee taxable in Arkansas?

The tax treatment of a dealer documentation fee in Arkansas is not confirmed in this guide. The answer can depend on how the charge is described and whether it is mandatory or bundled with the vehicle price. Use the calculator's editable tax toggle and confirm the invoice treatment with the state revenue agency.

Do county and city fees change a car's out-the-door price in Arkansas?

A buyer in or near Little Rock, Fayetteville, Bentonville may see different local taxes and registration costs from another part of Arkansas. Examples of relevant counties or jurisdictions include Pulaski, Benton, Washington.
