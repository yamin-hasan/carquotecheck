Privacy Policy

How CarQuoteCheck handles browser-only quote data, saved comparisons and shared links.

What this tool does

CarQuoteCheck is a client-side quote calculator. The information you type into the auditor is processed in your browser. The site does not require an account and does not operate a quote database or backend API.

Saved quotes and browser storage

When you save a quote, it is stored in your browser's local storage on that device. Saved quotes remain until you remove them, clear browser storage, or use a different browser or device. Anyone with access to your browser profile may be able to view them.

Shareable links

When you create a share link, the quote values are encoded in the URL fragment after the hash character. The fragment is not sent as part of a normal HTTP request to the website server, but anyone who receives the link can decode and view the quote values. Do not include information you would not share.

Third-party assets

The site loads fonts and icons from Fontshare, Google Fonts, and Cloudflare's CDN-hosted Font Awesome stylesheet. Those providers may receive standard technical request information such as your IP address and browser metadata when your browser requests their files. Review their privacy policies for details.

No analytics or advertising cookies

This generated MVP does not include analytics tags, ad pixels, remarketing scripts, or a cookie-based account system. Cloudflare Pages may process ordinary technical logs as part of hosting and security.

Children

The tool is intended for adults shopping for a vehicle and is not designed for children.

Changes

This notice may be updated as the site changes. Check this page for the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact details on the Contact page. The displayed email address must be configured with a monitored mailbox before public launch.
