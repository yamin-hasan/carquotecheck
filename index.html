COMMON QUESTIONS

Car-buying questions, answered

Plain-English context. Always confirm the final figures on your buyer's order.

What does out-the-door price mean?

The out-the-door (OTD) price is the total amount you must pay to complete the purchase, including the vehicle price, taxes, dealer fees, and government charges. It may be reduced by a trade-in or down payment depending on how the deal is structured.

Is a dealer documentation fee a government fee?

Usually no. A documentation or processing fee is charged by the dealership for paperwork. Some states regulate its amount or disclosure, but it is different from a state title or registration fee. Ask for every charge to be itemized on the buyer's order.

Does CarQuoteCheck provide legal or tax advice?

No. CarQuoteCheck is an educational estimate tool, not a law firm, tax adviser, DMV, or dealer. State and local rules change and may depend on the vehicle or transaction. Confirm the final tax, fee, and title amounts with the relevant agency before signing.

Does the calculator upload my quote?

No. Quote calculations run in your browser. Saved quotes use local browser storage, and share links encode the quote in the URL fragment. Do not share a link if it contains information you consider private; the tool does not need your name or VIN.
