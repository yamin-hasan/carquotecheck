About CarQuoteCheck

Our mission, calculation approach, public data sources and important limitations.

Built for the moment before you sign

CarQuoteCheck helps US car buyers understand a dealership's out-the-door (OTD) price. The tool separates the selling price, vehicle tax, dealer documentation fee, government title and registration charges, trade-in value, and optional add-ons.

How the audit works

The calculator estimates tax from the entered selling price, eligible trade-in credit, a taxable dealer doc fee, and taxable add-ons. It compares the dealer's quote with a negotiation target using a verified doc-fee cap where one is available, or a clearly labeled market benchmark when a cap is not confirmed. It then calculates a fairness score as an explanatory signal, not an official rating.

Data and sources

State vehicle-tax and trade-in references are based on OTDCheck's 51-jurisdiction dataset, last reviewed November 17, 2026. Its underlying values link to official state revenue, motor-vehicle, attorney-general, or legislative sources and are published under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence. Open the state fee and tax dataset.

Average dealer documentation-fee values are market benchmarks reproduced from CarEdge's 2026 state guide. They are not legal caps, official state averages, current dealer quotes, or guarantees of what any particular buyer will pay. Review the benchmark source.

Federal pricing-transparency context references the Federal Trade Commission's Automobile Industry Pricing Transparency FAQs.

What the tool cannot know

The calculator cannot determine every local tax, vehicle-weight formula, county registration fee, lender condition, dealer-specific disclosure, title eligibility issue, or exemption. Some state doc-fee and doc-fee-taxability rules are marked not verified rather than guessed. You should confirm the precise figures with the relevant state agency and the dealer's written buyer's order.

Privacy by design

No quote backend or user account is used in this MVP. Calculations run locally. Saved comparisons are kept in local browser storage, and share links include the quote in the URL fragment.

Corrections

State rules change. Please send a report with the state, fee name, amount or rule, source URL, and date observed through the Contact page.
